# Scope

## The Unique Kernel
Compare two fixed qubit preparations under different measurement directions. Equal outcome probabilities along Z do not make the preparations identical; X distinguishes these orthogonal states. Single trials and batches also separate theoretical probability from finite-sample frequency.

## Who It Is For
Complete quantum beginners, with a visual-first explanation and optional formulas.

## The Core Loop
Inspect the Bloch sphere, choose a measurement direction, prepare and measure fresh qubits, then compare A/B outcomes. View rotation is separate from measurement-axis editing.

## Feature Boundary
Fixed plus/minus preparations, arbitrary draggable measurement direction, X/Z shortcuts, one or 100 fresh trials per preparation, responsive particle-to-result illustrations, independent counts, cancellation, reduced-motion/off-screen fallback, and English explanations. A compact optional bit-to-qubit primer and genuine guided comparison/challenge support the core loop.

## What Working Looks Like
A beginner can see random outcomes with identical Z probabilities, switch to X to reveal deterministic separation, and explain that changing the viewpoint alone changes no probability. Each trial is a fresh preparation; animations are schematic outcomes rather than particle trajectories.

## Explicitly Excluded
No circuit editor, additional state preparations, maze solver, quantum-hardware connection, accounts, paid API, or generalized speedup claim.
